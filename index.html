The Story

We bought this Westy in 2004 with 84,000 miles and have owned it ever since. Between 2007 and 2010, we took it on multiple cross-country road trips - driving coast to coast and leaving it in airport long-term parking between adventures. After shipping it home from Seattle in 2011, we invested in a full engine, transmission, and suspension rebuild at 108,000 miles so it would be ready for anything. It served as a daily driver from 2012 to 2017 and has been mostly parked since. It's a great van that deserves to get back on the road.
